🇵🇱Korzeń

Learn Polish through its Sanskrit, Hindi, Latin & Greek roots

About Korzeń

Korzeń (root in Polish) is a flashcard app that links Polish words to roots you may already know from English, Hindi, Konkani, or Marathi.

Polish shares Indo-European roots with all of them. Pairs like matka/mā, trzy/tīn, and serce/hṛdaya aren’t coincidences. A word sticks deep when it connects to one you already know.

Word connections

The etymological bridge is a memory aid. Most links are real roots; some are mnemonics, not dictionary etymology.

How to study

  • 🃏Flip for meaning, phonetics, example, and connection.
  • 🔄Missed words become review drills.
  • 🔊Tap the speaker for pronunciation.
  • 🌐Filter by category or study all 238 words.

Build one for your language

Code is free and open source. Fork the repo, swap data/vocabulary.json, and host it.
